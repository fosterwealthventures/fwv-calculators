# Credit Utilization Optimizer: How to See Which Card to Pay First

Credit utilization is one of the clearest numbers you can control in a credit profile. It compares how much revolving credit you are using against your available limits.

## Why utilization matters

A person can have perfect payment history but still look risky if several cards are close to their limits. That is why both total utilization and card-by-card utilization matter.

## How to use the calculator

Use the [Credit Utilization Optimizer](/credit-utilization) to enter each card, current balance, limit, and planned payment. The calculator shows current utilization, after-payment utilization, and which card may deserve attention first.
