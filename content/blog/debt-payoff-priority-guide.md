# Debt Payoff Priority: Snowball vs Avalanche Without Spreadsheet Confusion

Debt payoff decisions can feel emotional because every balance competes for attention. A priority ranker helps you separate momentum from interest cost.

## Avalanche vs snowball

Avalanche usually prioritizes the highest interest rate first. Snowball prioritizes the smallest balance first. Neither strategy is automatically perfect for every person.
